# AVP (Alien)

Welcome to AVP (Alien)! This Minecraft mod is made in 1.21.1 and is home to alien-related content from the Alien, Predator and AVP franchises. The mod is currently available for Fabric and NeoForge.

---

## MIGRATING FROM "AVP"

If you are migrating from the 1.21.1 "AVP" mod (found here: https://modrinth.com/mod/avp) to the split up AVP files, you MUST use ALL modules provided (Alien, Human and Predator) to avoid losing data in your worlds and/or corrupting your worlds. Do not use the standalone AVP mod as it will no longer be updated.

Also, please **BACK UP YOUR WORLDS** before migrating! The different modules should migrate their respective content automatically, but it never hurts to back things up just in case! :)

## Features

### Xenomorph Lifecycle

Experience the complete xenomorph lifecycle with **12 distinct entity types** and **4 strain variants**:

**Lifecycle Stages:**
- **Ovomorph** - The iconic alien egg that waits patiently for hosts to approach.
- **Facehugger** - Parasitic stage that seeks out and attaches to viable hosts.
- **Chestburster** - The emerging larval stage after successful implantation.
- **Adolescent** - Transitional growth stage before reaching maturity.

**Adult Xenomorph Castes:**
- **Drone** - The standard xenomorph, versatile workers and warriors of the hive.
- **Warrior** - Combat-focused caste with enhanced offensive capabilities.
- **Runner** - Quadrupedal variant, faster and more agile than standard xenomorphs.
- **Prowler** - Stealthy hunters specialized in ambush tactics.
- **Spitter** - Ranged attackers capable of projecting acidic projectiles.
- **Boiler** - Volatile xenomorphs that explode in a shower of acid when killed.
- **Crusher** - Heavily armored tanks with devastating charging attacks.
- **Praetorian** - Elite royal guards, larger and more powerful than standard castes.
- **Queen** - The matriarch of the hive, capable of laying eggs via her ovipositor.
- **Predalien** - Hybrid xenomorphs born from yautja hosts, combining traits of both species.

**Strain Variants:**

Each xenomorph caste comes in multiple strain variants with unique appearances:
- **Standard** - Classic xenomorph coloration.
- **Nether** - Fire-resistant variants adapted to hellish environments.
- **Aberrant** - Mutated strain with distinct visual characteristics. Only available when the AVP: Human module is present.
- **Irradiated** - Radioactive variants found near contaminated areas. Only available when the AVP: Human module is present.
- **Royal** - Special lineage destined to become queens.

### Hive System

Xenomorphs form organized hives with intelligent group behaviors:

- **Hive Membership** - Xenomorphs belong to hives and coordinate their actions.
- **Leadership Hierarchy** - Hives select leaders based on caste priority (queens, praetorians, etc.).
- **Population Balancing** - Hives manage their caste composition for optimal efficiency.
- **Hive Merging** - Nearby hives can merge into larger, more powerful colonies.
- **Resin Spreading** - Xenomorphs spread resin to expand and reinforce their territory.

### Armor Sets

Protect yourself with **8 complete armor sets**, each with 4 pieces (helmet, chestplate, leggings, boots):

**Chitin Armor:**
- **Chitin Armor** - Basic xenomorph chitin provides decent protection.
- **Nether Chitin Armor** - Fire-resistant armor from nether xenomorphs.
- **Aberrant Chitin Armor** - Basic chitin armor from aberrant xenomorphs.
- **Irradiated Chitin Armor** - Armor crafted from irradiated xenomorph remains.

**Plated Chitin Armor:**
- **Plated Chitin Armor** - Reinforced chitin offering superior durability.
- **Plated Nether Chitin Armor** - Enhanced fire-resistant protection.
- **Plated Aberrant Chitin Armor** - Enhanced aberrant variant.
- **Plated Irradiated Chitin Armor** - Reinforced irradiated chitin gear.

### Building Materials

Over **100 building blocks** across multiple material types, each with full stair, slab, and wall variants:

**Resin Blocks**
- Resin, smooth resin, resin bricks, and ribbed resin patterns.
- Resin veins that spread organically across surfaces.
- Resin webs for creating atmospheric hive environments.
- Resin vents and nodes for functional hive structures.

**Chitin Blocks**
- Chitin blocks, polished chitin, and chitin bricks.
- Chiseled chitin bricks with decorative patterns.
- Chiseled chitin bricks with embryo designs.

**Variant Blocks**
- Complete block sets for nether, aberrant, and irradiated strains.
- Each variant maintains full stair, slab, and wall support.

### Resources & Crafting

**Chitin Materials:**
- Chitin from standard xenomorphs.
- Nether chitin (fire-resistant) from nether variants.
- Aberrant chitin from aberrant variants.
- Irradiated Chitin from irradiated variants.
- Plated versions of all chitin types for armor crafting.

**Resin Materials:**
- Resin balls for crafting resin blocks.
- Variant resin balls matching each xenomorph strain.

**Special Items:**
- **Royal Jelly** - Precious substance harvested from queens. Accelerates growth when used on aliens.
- **Poison Jelly** - Toxic variant with harmful effects. Stunts growth permanently when used on aliens.
- **Royal Jelly Block** - Decorative storage for royal jelly.

### Collectibles

- **Alien Music Disc** - Atmospheric soundtrack for your builds.
- **Music Disc Fragments** - Collectible pieces to assemble the full disc.
- **Pottery Sherds** - Four unique designs (ovoid, parasite, royalty, vector) for decorated pots.
